📊 HR Analytics & Employee Attrition Dashboard

An interactive executive Tableau dashboard designed to analyze workforce demographics, evaluate job satisfaction metrics, and identify key drivers behind employee attrition to support strategic HR retention initiatives.

🔗 **[View Live Interactive Dashboard on Tableau Public](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/afroj.khan/viz/HRtableauproject_17940787667390/Dashboard1)**

📌 Executive Summary & Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The primary goal of this dashboard is to provide HR leadership with actionable workforce insights. By monitoring headcount metrics and segmenting employee turnover across departments, age groups, and education backgrounds, organizations can proactively address attrition risks.

Core Metrics Tracked:

Total Employee Count: 1,470

Total Attrition Count: 237

Overall Attrition Rate: 16.12%

Active Employees: 1,233

Average Employee Age: 37 Years

📸 Dashboard Overview

🔍 Key Insights & Business Findings

Department-Wise Attrition Concentration:

R&D accounts for the largest proportion of total attrition at 56.12% (133 employees), followed by Sales at 38.82% (92 employees), while HR accounts for only 5.06% (12 employees).

Age & Experience Risk Factors:

Employees in the 25–34 age bracket experience the highest rate of turnover (29.11% / 112 employees), indicating potential challenges in early-career retention and career progression pathways.

Education Background Analysis:

The majority of exiting workforce members come from Life Sciences (89) and Medical (63) educational backgrounds.

Job Satisfaction & Role Impact:

Sales Executives (112) and Laboratory Technicians (80) exhibit high counts in lower job satisfaction tiers (Ratings 1 & 2), highlighting specific departments where engagement strategies need reinforcement.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

Tableau Public / Desktop: Interactive visualization, custom layout containers, KPI card design, dynamic filtering.

Calculated Fields & Bins: Built age group binning (Bin Size: 5), percentage of total calculations, and custom measure logic.

Data Preprocessing: Standardized demographic flags and handled missing values using Python (Pandas) / Excel prior to visualization.
